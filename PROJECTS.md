## Publication principles

- Only publish project code, client references, logos, screenshots and data when allowed by the applicable agreement or owner.
- Describe **shipped**, **in-development**, and **planned** functionality separately.
- Keep production credentials, payment details, customer records and private integrations out of public repositories.
- Avoid moving existing repositories before confirming Vercel/GitHub links, permissions, ownership and deployment integrations.
- Link company projects from personal technical case studies without duplicating source repositories unnecessarily.

## Case study process

1. Confirm publication permission.
2. Document the business problem and agreed scope.
3. List only verified delivered features.
4. Explain system architecture using sanitized diagrams.
5. Add approved screenshots or synthetic examples.
6. Attribute Cwlwm Systems' contribution accurately.
7. Record measurable outcomes only when evidence exists.
8. Document current status, next steps and support arrangements.
