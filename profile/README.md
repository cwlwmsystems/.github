## Who We Are

**Cwlwm Systems** is an independent software and digital solutions business founded by **Kasey Pelchy in August 2026**. We build practical websites, business applications, integrations and operational systems for local businesses and community organizations.

We focus on the entire workflow: understanding the business requirement, structuring the underlying data, implementing the software, validating results and documenting how the system is maintained.

## Services

| Area | What we deliver |
| --- | --- |
| Websites and digital presence | Responsive business websites, local search visibility, structured content, technical SEO |
| Custom applications | Customer and staff portals, dashboards, operational workflows and administrative tools |
| Ecommerce | Catalogs, checkout integrations, order management, loyalty and customer systems |
| Automation and integrations | API connections, webhooks, notifications and cross-system data flows |
| Data and reporting | Structured data models, import pipelines, reporting and operational visibility |
